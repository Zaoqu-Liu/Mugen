## Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Review and merge pull requests in a timely manner
- Triage issues and label them appropriately
- Ensure CI stays green on the main branch
- Make release decisions and publish releases
- Uphold the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Becoming a Maintainer

The path to maintainership:

1. **Consistent contributions** — Multiple merged PRs demonstrating deep understanding of the codebase
2. **Code quality** — Contributions follow project style, include tests, and are well-documented
3. **Community engagement** — Helpful in issues, reviews other PRs constructively
4. **Trust** — The BDFL has confidence in your judgment and technical decisions

Nomination is by invitation from the BDFL. There is no formal application process — just keep contributing quality work.
